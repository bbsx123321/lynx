PadShift — 3D controller asset provenance
========================================

Status: the main-window controller is a downloaded CC0 model, with the original procedural
controller kept as the automatic fallback.

Bundled model
-------------
* Folder: assets/3d/dualsense-cc0/
* Model: Sony DualSense controller by CasperH2O (3DControllerModels)
* Source: https://github.com/CasperH2O/3DControllerModels
* Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public-domain dedication)
* Downloaded: 2026-10-18
* Files: source.blend (raw source), dualsense.obj + dualsense.mtl (import-ready conversion),
  LICENSE.txt (full provenance and conversion notes).
* Attribution is not required by CC0; the author/licence are credited in Preferences -> 关于.
* Why DualSense: it models real stick caps (dished top + wall + tapered shaft) that tilt
  directly. The previously bundled Xbox One asset models its stick objects as the recessed
  wells, which tilted as sockets and had to be worked around.

Procedural fallback
-------------------
If assets/3d/dualsense-cc0 is missing or cannot be parsed, the view automatically falls back to
src/PadShift.App/Infrastructure/GamepadMeshBuilder.cs (original geometry, same licence as the
rest of PadShift). This path is covered by tests/PadShift.Tests/GamepadModelTests.cs and
VisualAssetTests.cs.

Renderer
--------
WPF Viewport3D (System.Windows.Media.Media3D) with a hand-written OBJ/MTL importer
(src/PadShift.App/Infrastructure/GamepadModelLoader.cs). No NuGet package, no textures.
