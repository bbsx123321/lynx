Conversion performed for PadShift
----------------------------------
The per-part OBJ files in parts/ were merged into xbox360.obj + xbox360.mtl by
tools/model-merge-3dco.py (pure Python, no Blender needed). The script:
  * maps every part file to the PadShift control groups (A/B/X/Y in Xbox semantics, sticks
    as stick+cap+ring assemblies that tilt together, shells/decorations onto Body);
  * applies each part's assembly offset from info.json (stick/cap reuse their ring's
    offset);
  * rotates the flat-lying CAD pose into PadShift's display pose (X right, Y up, Z towards
    the camera) without mirroring.

Scene budget: 7749 triangles, target width 29 scene units. No textures are used.

Raw parts: parts/ (unchanged) + xbox360.obj + xbox360.mtl.
